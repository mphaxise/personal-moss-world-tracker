# Idea Strategy

Date: 2026-03-15
Status: historical origin plus current interpretation

## Original backlog idea

Original source fields:
- Title: `Personal Moss World Tracker`
- Rank: 3
- Priority: 5
- Source: manual backlog
- Review date: 2026-02-24

Original rationale:
- turns everyday moss sightings into a lightweight community discovery product with visual browsing and location context

Original first milestone:
- ship a single-page prototype with one photo submission flow and toggleable card and map views

Original end-of-day target:
- working one-page moss tracker with seeded entries and an iNaturalist enrichment spike documented

## What happened after exploration

The original milestone was built as a prototype, but later founder review narrowed the better wedge.

The project direction changed because:
- `SF Trees` clarified the stronger reference model: neighborhood guide, editorial voice, walking tours, and provenance
- Bernal Heights emerged as the right first neighborhood
- ecology research showed the content should be broader than strict moss while keeping a simple public-facing brand
- the product opportunity looked stronger as an atlas than as a community submission utility

## Current interpretation

The active interpretation of the idea is now:
- `Bernal Heights Moss Atlas`
- one curated walk
- 8 to 12 editorial stops
- static-first MVP
- public-facing `moss atlas`, internally informed by mosses, liverworts, and lichens

## Why this interpretation is stronger

It preserves what was good in the original idea:
- visual discovery
- place context
- lightweight, local feel

It removes what was weak in the original framing:
- broad community-tool scope before proving editorial value
- infrastructure-heavy submission surfaces too early
- weak retention assumptions for anonymous contributors
